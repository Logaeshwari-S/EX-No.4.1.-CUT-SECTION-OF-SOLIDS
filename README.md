# EX No.4.1. CUT SECTION OF SOLIDS
## DATE:

## AIM
To create the solid model from given drawing and section the solid model

## REQUIREMENTS: 
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3.16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE:
1.First choose create sketch and choose top view. draw a straight construct line, to create the base, draw a circle of radius 10mm,15mm and 25mm having the same center point.

2.Draw a straight line of length 70 mm passing through the centre.

<img width="393" height="260" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/12c7f9b1-ba94-4dcc-b818-53f4014e7180" />

3.Draw two circles of diameter 12mm and 30mm from the right most point of the line of length 70mm.

4.Now draw a tangent line joining  the centre circle of radius 25mm and right outermost circle of radius 15 and mirror them to the left as well.

5.trim the unnecessary sketches and extrude the base for 10mm and extrude the small circle in each side of the base inward as it creates a hole.

<img width="246" height="276" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e0d7355a-15a2-4d08-bc1d-c3031b18ee93" />

6.Now choose the area between the area of the centre circle of radius 10mm and 15mm and extrude it by 22mm.

7.Now choose the area between the area of the centre circle of radius 15mm and 25mm and extrude it by 28mm.

<img width="395" height="287" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/000ef885-98b3-4339-aba3-6ca602f5dd28" />

8.Now choose finish sketch and choose the create sketch and choose the right view and draw a circle of radius of 6 mm.

<img width="310" height="270" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/78dd8101-b62b-4be4-8b1f-c50193b56d3a" />
